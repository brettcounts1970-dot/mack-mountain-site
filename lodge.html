The Retreat

Everything you need, high above it all.

Perched on a private ridge across twenty acres, Mack Mountain Lodge pairs a warm, hand-built cabin feel with room for the whole group to spread out. Vaulted pine ceilings, a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, walls of glass framing the Ouachitas — and out back, a private hot tub, fire pit, and covered pavilion built for sunsets. The whole ridge looks out over Daisy State Park and Lake Greeson.

The Great Room

A fireplace worth gathering around.

The heart of the lodge: a soaring stone fireplace, deep leather seating, and cathedral windows that pull the mountains right inside. Open to the kitchen and loft, it's where mornings start and evenings wind down.

The Kitchen

Cook for a crowd.

A full chef's kitchen in knotty alder and granite, anchored by a big island with bar seating and an antler chandelier overhead. Plenty of room to prep, serve, and gather before heading out to the deck.

Four Bedrooms

Room for everyone to rest.

Four thoughtfully styled bedrooms — from the tufted-leather master suite to the western room with its barrel nightstands — each finished in warm pine with quality linens. Comfortable sleeping for the whole group across three full baths.

The Loft

Game room & media loft.

Upstairs is built for downtime: a pool table and full-length shuffleboard on one end, and a media room on the other with an extra-large TV and theater-style seating for movie nights. Fast Wi-Fi keeps the gamers happy too — all under a dramatic A-frame window wall.

Stay in Rhythm

A real home gym.

Keep your routine on the mountain. A dedicated fitness building with a full rack, free weights, and cardio — and when you throw open the roll-up doors, you're training to a wide-open view over the Ouachitas and Lake Greeson.

The Outdoors

Porches, decks, hot tub & fire pit.

The best rooms here don't have walls. A full wraparound porch and a large back deck — complete with an outdoor fireplace and plenty of room to entertain — wrap the lodge in living space. Soak in the private hot tub as the ridge lights up, circle the fire pit after dark, and claim a chair on the covered pavilion for a front-row seat to the sunset.
