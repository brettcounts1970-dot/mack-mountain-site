The Lodge

Made for gathering, built to unwind.

Step inside to vaulted pine ceilings, a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, and walls of glass that pull the mountains right into the great room. There's a full kitchen for cooking together, four comfortable bedrooms across three baths, and a game-room loft with pool and shuffleboard for after the sun goes down.

Outside, the ridge is yours. Soak in the private hot tub, circle the fire pit under the stars, and claim a chair on the covered pavilion for the evening light — with ATV trails from the door and Lake Greeson just minutes away.

The Setting

Lake Greeson & the Ouachita Mountains.

Crystal water, pine-covered ridges, and some of the best fishing and water sports in Arkansas — all right outside the door. The lodge looks out over Daisy State Park and the lake: cast a line at the marina, hit the water, ride the trails, or drive over to Crater of Diamonds State Park and dig for the real thing.
